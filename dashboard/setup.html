Guardrail set up limits for your AI trading agent

Give your AI agent a perps account, with limits it cannot trade past.

On Solana devnet, with test dollars. Your wallet signs two transactions and keeps its keys. You set the limits; the agent gets a key that can trade only inside them and can never withdraw. About 0.06 devnet SOL in all.

  1. Connect your wallet

    Your wallet becomes the owner: the only key that can change the limits, swap the agent or trade directly. Switch it to devnet first.

  2. Open a Velocity account with test dollars

    Velocity is a perps exchange on Solana, Drift's successor. This creates your account there and deposits 1,000 test dUSDT from Velocity's devnet faucet. One signature.

    Connect a wallet first.

  3. Set the limits

    Pick a starting point, then change any number. Every order the agent sends is checked against these on chain.

    Fixed on this page: market SOL-PERP only; limit and oracle orders (no market orders); the agent can always reduce its position inside the price band and cancel its orders. Velocity's devnet oracle is weeks old, so the oracle-age limit is set very loose here (100,000,000 slots); on a live market you would set it tight.

    • Give your agent a key

      This key can place and cancel orders through the guard and nothing else. It cannot withdraw or change a limit.

      The file is the agent's secret key. It never leaves this browser except as this download, and is not stored anywhere. Keep it where your agent runs.

    • Create the guard

      One signature: the guard program becomes your Velocity account's trading delegate with these limits, your wallet is set as the daily loss keeper, and the agent gets its fee money.

    • Connect your agent

      Add this to your agent's MCP settings. It gets three tools: place an order, cancel, and read the limits. There is no tool to change them.

      Appears when the guard exists.

    Devnet only. Transactions go through Solana's public devnet RPC. The owner can always trade directly, change the limits or remove the guard.
